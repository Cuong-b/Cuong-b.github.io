About

My name is Cuong Bui (I pronouce it Kung Boo·ee). I graduated from UC Berkeley with a BA in Astrophysics and Physics and I'm keenly interested in understanding how things and systems work, figuring out how they can be improved, and learning from the process of making.

Through my background, I've gained experience working on data analytics, product development, software, and business. My work has spanned teaching and tutoring to business consulting and developing internal tooling. Overall, I enjoy work that flexes both the techinical and creative sides of my brain. Since graduating, I've also become increasingly drawn to work that feels tangible and connected to people and the communities around me.

Off the clock, I enjoy cooking, hiking, playing competitive pool, colorguard, and woodworking. I've been competing in various colorguard and marching band circuits for at least 10 years now. Through colorguard, I've had the priviledge of performing in front of stadium audiences, travel across 37 different states, and be part of a team featured on the New York Times! Recently, I've been diving into the world of woodworking. My favorite practice so far has been turning wood on the lathe and creating beautiful bowls and cups. I'm also super curious to explore complex joinery as I continue to learn. On top of that, I co-teach a wood ring making course at my local woodshop along with my partner.
